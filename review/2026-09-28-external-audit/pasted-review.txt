I made no edits to the project. I ran two replays in throwaway containers, and a helper parsed the recorded feed data; all output went to my scratch folder.

Bottom line: the code is careful, but the result is economic: no edge. The engine's cycle returns matched an independent recalculation to within 0.22 bps on every route that could be priced. Re-running the verified replay of the smoke run gave an identical result. Over 48 minutes live plus the 10-minute smoke run, the best cycle after fees was −10.5 bps even with no freshness filter (−12.3 bps with the 1-second filter), and was never above zero. Every cycle needs at least two full 7-bps taker legs plus a 1.4-bps stablecoin leg, so fees alone cost about 15 bps. That matches this morning's Binance result. The fixes below improve measurement quality; they are unlikely to change that conclusion.

Problems that distort the measurements

1. The freshness rule doesn't match how Hyperliquid's feed works (book.rs:174-207, hyperliquid.rs:86-91)
   - The bbo channel only sends when the bes market is unchanged, not stale. 12 of the 29 markets sent no bbo message at all in 48 minutes.
   - The default l2Book feed is a snapshot of all 29 markets pushed together every ~5.4 s. A 1-second depth limit therefore passes only 18.6% of the time
   - For HYPE, the depth snapshot matched the current best bid/ask only 4.6% of the time. Any paper trade would mostly end "unobservable".
   - Fix: subscribe with fast: true (about 0rliquid docs and a live probe).
2. 180 of the 210 routes are dead. They pass through nine USDH-quoted markets whose books were empty in every           snapshot, with zero volume. That is exactonly 30 routes with prices. Some of theremaining 30 also use junk books (@152, @212). Empty markets should be dropped at discovery.
3. The fill model ignores feed delay (paper.rs:162-171). An order arriving at time t is filled against the book this machine had received by t, which is the exchange's book one feed delay earlier. That flatters opportunities that are fading. Fix: fill against the book received at confirmation time. That adds no look-ahead, because the fill is only revealed then.                                                                                               4. Size estimates are biased low, badly at s66, 218-222, 270-273).
   - The fee is charged in the received token, so a buy leaves "N lots minus a tiny fee", and the next sell rounds down to N−1 lots.                                                                                                  - The leftover lot is valued at zero. Forth about $0.85, which costs roughly 350 bpsat 25 USDC and about 9 bps at 1,000 USDC.
   - The 25/100/250 USDC sizes can almost never clear the 5-bps entry threshold. Better: value leftovers at the bid,      in a separate field.
5. Each leg waits for confirmation before the next, so a triangle takes at least 1.5 s. This mostly measures the risk of holding one leg while the next is pending. That only matters if a profitable price ever appears.
